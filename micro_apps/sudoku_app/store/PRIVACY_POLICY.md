## Summary

Sudoku works fully offline. We do not operate a backend server, we do
not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address,
or any other directly-identifying information. The only data processing
that happens is what's required to show advertising, process an optional
one-time in-app purchase, and (in regions where required) collect your
advertising consent choice — all handled by Google's own SDKs, not by
custom code we wrote.

## Information we do not collect

- No account or sign-in — the App has none.
- No name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
- No contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location access.
- No user-generated content (nothing you type is sent anywhere).

## Information stored only on your device

The App saves the following locally, using standard Android/iOS app
storage. It is never transmitted to us, because we have no server to
send it to:

- Your app language selection
- Your selected difficulty
- Puzzle progress (which cells you've filled in, which puzzles you've
  completed)
- Sound/vibration preferences
- Whether you've purchased Remove Ads
- Whether you've completed first-time setup

Uninstalling the App removes all of this.

## Information processed by third-party services

### Advertising — Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

The App shows banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads via Google
AdMob, unless you've purchased Remove Ads. To do this, Google's SDK may
process:

- Advertising identifier (subject to your device's ad tracking settings)
- Approximate location (derived from IP address, for ad relevance and
  fraud prevention)
- Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version)

This is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and, for
ad personalization specifically,
[Google's Ads Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### Advertising consent — Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Where required by law (for example, users in the EEA, UK, or
Switzerland), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for
your consent before showing personalized ads. This determines your
approximate region (to know whether consent is required) and stores your
consent choice. You can change this choice at any time from
**Settings → Ad privacy options**.

### Purchases — Google Play Billing

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase ($1.00, one-time) is processed
entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment
card details — that's handled by Google Play. We only receive
confirmation that the purchase was completed, which we store locally on
your device to know ads should be turned off.
