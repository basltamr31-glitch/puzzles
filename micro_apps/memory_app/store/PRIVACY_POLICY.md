# Privacy Policy — Memory Match

_Last updated: 14 September 2026_

BT Developer ("we", "us") operates the
Memory Match mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains what data the
App processes and why.

This policy is provided in English only.

## Summary

The App works fully offline. We do not operate a server, we do not
require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or
any other information that identifies you personally. The only data
processing that happens is what is needed to show advertising, to process
an optional one-time in-app purchase, and — in regions where the law
requires it — to record your advertising consent choice. All of that is
handled by Google's own SDKs, not by code we wrote.

## Information we do not collect

- No account or sign-in. The App has none.
- No name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
- No access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location.
- No user-generated content. Nothing you enter in a puzzle is sent
  anywhere.

## Information stored only on your device

The App saves the following using standard Android and iOS app storage.
It is never transmitted to us, because we have no server to send it to:

- Your app language selection
- Your appearance choice (system, light, or dark)
- Your selected difficulty
- Puzzle progress: which cards you have already matched and which you
  have turned over, which puzzles you have completed, and how long you
  have spent on the puzzle you are currently playing
- Your rewards: stars earned, puzzles solved, your current and best
  daily streak, and which badges you have unlocked
- Sound effect, vibration, and background music preferences
- Whether play reminders are switched on, and the time you last opened a
  puzzle (used only to decide whether to send that reminder)
- Whether you have purchased Remove Ads
- Whether you have completed first-time setup

Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

## Notifications

If you switch on **play reminders** in Settings, the App schedules a
reminder on your device to nudge you to come back and play. These
notifications are generated entirely on your device. There is no push
service, no server, and no device token is created or transmitted. The
setting is off unless you turn it on, and turning it off cancels any
pending reminder.

## Information processed by third-party services

### Advertising — Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

Unless you have purchased Remove Ads, the App shows banner,
interstitial, and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To do this,
Google's SDK may process:

- Your device's advertising identifier, subject to your device's ad
  tracking settings
- Approximate location derived from your IP address, for ad relevance
  and fraud prevention
- Device and app information such as device model, operating system
  version, and app version

This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy) and, for ad personalization
specifically, Google's Ads Privacy & Terms
(https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

Ads require an internet connection. Playing puzzles does not.

### Advertising consent — Google User Messaging Platform

Where the law requires it — for example for users in the European
Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland — the App uses Google's
User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before showing
personalized ads. This determines your approximate region, in order to
know whether consent is required, and stores your choice. You can change
that choice at any time from **Settings → Ad privacy options**.

No ad is requested, and the advertising SDK is not started, until that
consent step has completed.

### Purchases — Google Play Billing

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is a one-time purchase processed
entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card
details; those are handled by Google Play. We receive only confirmation
that the purchase completed, which is stored on your device so the App
knows to stop showing ads.

### Analytics

The App does **not** transmit analytics. Product events such as "puzzle
completed" are written to the local debug log during development only,
and no analytics data leaves your device.

If this ever changes, this section and the Google Play Data Safety
declaration must both be updated to name the provider and state what it
collects.

## Age requirement

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at
children under 13, and its Google Play target audience is declared
accordingly.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including
children: the App has no account, and it does not collect names, email
addresses, phone numbers, or any other information that directly
identifies a user.

If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about the App's
privacy practices, contact us at baladream.help@gmail.com.

## Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

We are established in Finland, so the EU General Data Protection
Regulation applies to this App.

- **Personalized advertising** is processed on the basis of your
  **consent**, collected through Google's User Messaging Platform before
  any ad is requested. You may withdraw it at any time from
  **Settings → Ad privacy options**; withdrawal does not affect
  processing carried out before you withdrew.
- **Non-personalized advertising and fraud prevention** rely on our
  **legitimate interest** in funding a free app, balanced against the
  limited data involved.
- **Your purchase**, if you make one, is processed by Google Play as
  necessary to **perform the contract** for that purchase.

The data controller is BT Developer. For the advertising and payment processing described
above, Google acts as an independent controller of that data under its
own privacy policy.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have
it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to
data portability.

We hold no personal data ourselves — everything this App stores stays on
your device — so in practice there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct
or erase on your behalf; uninstalling the App removes it all. Requests
concerning advertising or purchase data should be directed to Google,
which holds that data as its own controller.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory
authority. In Finland that is the Office of the Data Protection
Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto), tietosuoja.fi.
